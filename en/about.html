02 References

I learned to look through the films of Hirokazu Kore-eda.

His films find what matters in small things: a family meal, a passing train, a conversation in a low voice. There is no artifice to distract you; there is time, natural light and attention.

I try to work the same way with brands. Before adding anything, I look for the image that is already there and give it the framing and the time it needs.

  • Japanese editorial design

    Plenty of white space, vertical composition and a single colour used sparingly.

  • Swiss Style

    A strict grid: every image takes the place that belongs to it.

03 How I work

01/04

Four steps, one person from start to finish.

  1. 01

    I listen

    Before proposing anything, I understand what the brand needs to publish, where and for whom.

  2. 02

    I direct

    I propose the concept, the formats and the production plan. I decide what is needed and what is not.

  3. 03

    I produce

    Photo and video in a single session, with the same mind behind every image.

  4. 04

    I deliver

    I handle the post-production and deliver every piece ready to publish, in all the formats you are going to use.
