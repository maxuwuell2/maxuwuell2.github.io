01 Services

Madrid, Spain

I direct your brand's image from start to finish.

I work with brands and businesses that need agency-level photo and video, without the slowness or the cost of a production company. You have a single point of contact, creative direction is included and you receive every piece ready to publish.

02 What I do

Light at 09:00

Six ways of working and one way of seeing.

Every project starts with understanding what the brand needs to publish. From there I decide the format, the production and the delivery.

Keep scrolling →

01Light at 09:00

Brand shoot

A single photo and video production, planned from the start for every format you are going to publish. It is my main service and the starting point of almost all my projects.

Includes

  • Concept and visual script before the shoot
  • Photo and video on the same day
  • Post-production of every piece
  • Delivery in every format

For whom

Brands that need to renew their image in one go, and consistently.

02Light at 11:00

Monthly content

A monthly retainer for brands that publish often. I plan the visual calendar, produce every month and keep the look consistent over time.

Includes

  • A visual calendar for each month
  • Monthly photo and video production
  • Pieces for social media, web and campaigns
  • A review of the results at the end of each month

For whom

Brands that publish every week and don't want to lose consistency.

03Light at 13:00

Advertising production

Campaigns with script, casting and direction. I coordinate whatever team is needed and take responsibility for the result from start to finish.

Includes

  • Concept and script
  • Casting and locations
  • Team coordination
  • Direction on set and post-production

For whom

Campaigns with a launch date and a clear message.

04Light at 15:00

Architecture and interiors

Photo and video of spaces for studios, hotels and hospitality. I work with the light of each place so the image explains the project.

Includes

  • Photography of spaces
  • Walkthrough video
  • Working with the light of the place
  • Delivery for web, press and competitions

For whom

Architecture and interior design studios, hotels and hospitality.

05Light at 17:00

Portrait

Professional and model portraits. I direct the session so the result feels natural and works in every format where it will be used.

Includes

  • Direction during the session
  • Professional and model portraits
  • Selection and retouching
  • Formats for web, social media and press

For whom

Professionals, teams and models who need an image of their own.

06Light at 19:00

Creative direction and post-production

I join agency and studio projects to bring judgement, post-production and multi-format adaptation.

Includes

  • Creative direction for agencies
  • Photo and video post-production
  • Multi-format adaptation
  • Colour grading and retouching

For whom

Agencies and studios that outsource production.
